Chickpea harvest bowl

Roasted sweet potato, chickpeas, and crisp vegetables meet a lemon-tahini dressing. An everyday bowl you can make your own.

30 minutes · 2 servings · Lunch

Ingredients

  • 1 medium sweet potato, cut into small cubes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1/2 yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2–3 tbsp water
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the sweet potato with olive oil and a pinch of salt. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for about 20 minutes, turning once, until tender.
  2. While the potato cooks, rinse the chickpeas, wash the vegetables, and prepare the greens, avocado, tomatoes, cabbage, and pepper.
  3. Whisk tahini and lemon juice together. Add water a spoonful at a time until pourable, then season to taste.
  4. Divide everything between two bowls. Add the warm sweet potato and spoon over the dressing.

Kitchen note: Use leftover roasted vegetables to make this an almost no-cook lunch.

Lemon salmon & quinoa

Flaky salmon with quinoa, green beans, and sweet tomatoes. Cook the grains while the salmon roasts to bring everything together.

30 minutes · 2 servings · Dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (about 5 oz each)
  • 1/2 cup dry quinoa, rinsed
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cups green beans, trimmed
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, half juiced and half sliced
  • 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Bring quinoa and water to a boil in a saucepan. Cover, reduce the heat, and simmer for about 15 minutes. Rest off the heat for 5 minutes.
  2. Place salmon and green beans on a lined baking sheet. Drizzle with oil and lemon juice, then season.
  3. Roast for 12–18 minutes depending on the thickness of the fish. Check that the salmon reaches 145°F (63°C) with a food thermometer.
  4. Fluff the quinoa and divide between two bowls. Add salmon, green beans, tomatoes, pumpkin seeds, and lemon slices.

Kitchen note: Frozen green beans work here too. Follow their package cooking instructions.

Includes or may include: Fish. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Fish: 145°F (63°C). Check the thickest part with a food thermometer. FoodSafety.gov guidance

Avocado & beet hummus toast

Earthy beet hummus and creamy avocado on crisp sourdough, finished with peppery greens and a squeeze of lemon.

10 minutes · 2 servings · Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 4 slices sourdough bread
  • 1/2 cup prepared beet hummus
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Toast the bread until crisp around the edges.
  2. Spread each slice with beet hummus.
  3. Top with avocado, arugula, and tomatoes.
  4. Finish with lemon juice and black pepper. Serve straight away.

Kitchen note: Regular hummus works just as well if beet hummus is not available.

Includes or may include: Wheat, Sesame. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Cherry tomato & basil pasta

Warm cherry tomatoes make their own simple sauce. A handful of basil and a little cooking water bring this easy pasta together.

20 minutes · 2 servings · Dinner

Ingredients

  • 6 oz dry farfalle pasta
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup grated vegetarian hard cheese
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in boiling water according to the package. Reserve 1/2 cup cooking water before draining.
  2. Meanwhile, warm olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook garlic for 30 seconds, then add tomatoes and a pinch of salt.
  3. Cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring, until the tomatoes soften. Add a splash of pasta water.
  4. Toss in the pasta, cheese, and basil. Add more cooking water if needed, season, and serve.

Kitchen note: Choose cheese made with vegetarian rennet for the vegetarian version.

Includes or may include: Wheat, Milk. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Golden red lentil soup

Red lentils soften into a silky soup with carrot, onion, and cumin. A squeeze of lemon keeps every spoonful bright.

35 minutes · 4 servings · Lunch

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 4 cups low-sodium vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large saucepan. Add onion and carrot and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add garlic and cumin. Stir for 30 seconds, then add lentils and stock.
  3. Bring to a gentle boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for about 20 minutes, until the lentils are soft.
  4. Take off the heat. Carefully blend with an immersion blender until smooth. Stir in lemon juice, season, and finish with paprika.

Kitchen note: Add extra stock or water if you prefer a thinner soup.

Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Ginger chicken & rice

Gently poached chicken, fragrant ginger rice, and cool cucumber make a satisfying everyday dinner.

40 minutes · 2 servings · Dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 small boneless chicken breasts (about 12 oz total)
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken stock
  • 1-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, lightly crushed
  • 3/4 cup dry jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Fresh cilantro, to finish

Method

  1. Bring the stock, ginger, and garlic to a gentle simmer. Add the chicken and simmer gently for about 15–20 minutes, until a thermometer reads 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part. Remove to a clean plate and cover.
  2. Meanwhile, cook the rice in a separate pan using water and the timing and ratio on its package.
  3. Mix soy sauce with lime juice. Wash and slice the cucumber and tomatoes.
  4. Slice the cooked chicken. Serve with rice, vegetables, cilantro, and the sauce.

Kitchen note: Slice the ginger thinly so it flavors the stock quickly.

Includes or may include: Soy, Wheat. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Chicken: 165°F (74°C). Wash hands and utensils after handling raw poultry. FoodSafety.gov guidance

Chicken & broccoli coconut curry

Chicken and broccoli simmer in a gently spiced coconut sauce. Add lime at the end for a fresh finish.

30 minutes · 3 servings · Dinner

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tbsp mild red curry paste
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) light coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Fresh cilantro, to finish
  • Cooked rice, to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add chicken and cook for about 4 minutes, turning the pieces.
  2. Stir in the curry paste, then add coconut milk and water. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add broccoli and pepper. Simmer for 10–12 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) in the center.
  4. Finish with lime juice and cilantro. Serve as it is or with rice prepared separately.

Kitchen note: Curry pastes vary in heat and ingredients. Start small and check the label for allergens.

Includes or may include: Check curry paste label. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Chicken: 165°F (74°C), measured with a food thermometer. FoodSafety.gov guidance

Summer fruit yogurt bowl

Cool, creamy yogurt with seasonal fruit, chopped walnuts, and a sprinkle of chia. Easy to assemble and easy to change with the season.

5 minutes · 2 servings · Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 peach, sliced
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1 cup strawberries, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts
  • 2 tsp chia seeds

Method

  1. Wash and slice the peach and strawberries. Peel and slice the banana.
  2. Spoon yogurt into two bowls.
  3. Arrange the fruit on top and finish with walnuts and chia seeds.

Kitchen note: Use any fruit you have on hand. Frozen berries work once thawed.

Includes or may include: Milk, Tree nuts. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Berry & kiwi oatmeal

Warm oats topped with juicy berries and kiwi. A small spoonful of almond butter adds richness without extra effort.

15 minutes · 2 servings · Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups unsweetened oat drink
  • 1 cup mixed berries
  • 1 kiwi, peeled and sliced
  • 2 tbsp almond butter
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Combine oats, oat drink, and cinnamon in a small saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5–8 minutes, stirring, until creamy. Add a splash of water if needed.
  3. Divide between bowls. Top with washed berries, kiwi, and almond butter.

Kitchen note: Swap almond butter for sunflower seed butter if that suits your needs.

Includes or may include: Tree nuts. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Eggs & greens on toast

Toasted English muffins with eggs, tomatoes, and a tangy yogurt-mustard sauce, served with a handful of greens.

20 minutes · 2 servings · Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 2 English muffins, split
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 2 cups mixed greens, washed
  • 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Stir yogurt, mustard, lemon juice, and pepper together in a small bowl.
  2. Toast the English muffin halves. Top each with a slice of tomato.
  3. Bring a shallow pan of water to a gentle simmer. Crack eggs separately into a cup, then slide into the water. Poach until whites and yolks are firm, about 6–8 minutes depending on size.
  4. Lift the eggs out with a slotted spoon. Place on the muffins, add sauce, and serve with greens.

Kitchen note: A fully cooked fried egg is an easy alternative to poaching.

Includes or may include: Eggs, Wheat, Milk, Mustard. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Cook eggs until the yolk and white are firm. FoodSafety.gov guidance

Rainbow rice-paper rolls

Crisp vegetables and rice wrapped in delicate rice paper, with a quick peanut-lime dip. Start with cooked, cooled rice.

25 minutes · 2 servings · Lunch

Ingredients

  • 8 rice-paper wrappers
  • 1 cup cooked rice, cooled
  • 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1 cup lettuce, shredded
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint and cilantro
  • 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2–3 tbsp warm water

Method

  1. Wash and cut the vegetables. Arrange them beside the rice and herbs.
  2. Whisk peanut butter, lime juice, soy sauce, and warm water into a smooth dip.
  3. Soften one wrapper at a time in warm water according to the package, then lay on a damp board.
  4. Place a little rice, vegetables, and herbs near the bottom. Fold the sides inward and roll firmly. Repeat and serve with the dip.

Kitchen note: Do not overfill the wrappers. A little space makes them easier to roll.

Includes or may include: Peanuts, Soy, Wheat. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.

Loaded sweet potato & black beans

Roasted sweet potato wedges with black beans, tomato salsa, avocado, and a lime-tahini drizzle.

40 minutes · 2 servings · Dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into thin wedges
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tbsp water
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss sweet potato wedges with olive oil, paprika, and a pinch of salt. Spread on a baking sheet.
  2. Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning halfway, until tender. Meanwhile, warm the black beans in a small pan with a splash of water.
  3. Mix tahini, lime juice, and water into a dressing. Wash the greens and chop the tomatoes and avocado.
  4. Divide the greens and wedges between bowls. Add beans, tomatoes, avocado, and dressing.

Kitchen note: Spread the wedges in one layer so they roast instead of steam.

Includes or may include: Sesame. Check packaged ingredient labels for your needs.
